2nd Swing Trade-In Guide

Clubs Shafts Tech + More

Four quick steps from search to payout.

Step 1 of 4

Search, add, and check out.

Find your clubs by brand, model, or club type. Add each one to your trade-in cart, then hit Checkout.

  • Have a promo code? Current trade-in promos are listed on our Terms & Conditions page (linked at the bottom of 2ndSwing.com) or on the Trade + Sell landing page.
  • Adding accessories or multiple clubs? They all go in one cart.
Step 2 of 4

Pick how you get paid.

Store Credit pays the highest value. Prefer cash instead? Credit card, check, e-check, and PayPal are all available too.

  • Your total updates instantly as you switch options.
  • Cash payouts run about 10% below the Store Credit value.
  • Credit Card refund will be reserved for those golfers that are purchasing clubs today as well.
Step 3 of 4

We cover shipping.

Add a prepaid FedEx label for $9.99 — deducted from your payout, never charged extra. Good for up to 15 clubs in one box.

  • Review your order, accept the terms, and submit.
  • Print your packing list and shipping label right from the confirmation page.
Box it up: packing list inside, label outside, then drop it at any FedEx location.
Don't have a dedicated FedEx facility near you? Most local drug stores will have a FedEx drop off as well.
